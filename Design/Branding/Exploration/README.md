# Geet identity exploration

Four initial directions for Geet, the native Mac music player. Geet means song in Hindi and Urdu. The identity should feel quiet, expressive, and familiar enough to live in the menu bar every day.

The Listener direction was selected. The [finished asset package](../Listener/README.md) contains the symbol-only logo and app icon. The text in the exploration boards is not part of the final logo.

The reference is the [Hermes Agent identity](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/), inspected on 21 October 2026. Its reduced black-and-white portrait, strong silhouette, and classical character inform this exploration. Each Geet symbol is a new concept.

## The Singer

[Concept image](01-singer.png) · [Generation prompt](01-singer-prompt.txt)

A singer's profile with a lifted chin and a flowing contour that suggests humming. This is the closest response to the portrait reference and the most direct human expression of the name.

Proposed palette: ink `#20211C`, warm ivory `#F4F0E7`, and oxblood. Pair the emblem with a warm, restrained serif wordmark.

## The Songbird

[Concept image](02-songbird.png) · [Generation prompt](02-songbird-prompt.txt)

A compact singing bird, with a curved breast and a sweeping wing. The idea connects song to a recognizable everyday companion and gives Geet a potential character of its own.

Proposed palette: ink `#20211C`, warm ivory `#F4F0E7`, and forest `#355A48`.

## The Lyre

[Concept image](03-lyre.png) · [Generation prompt](03-lyre-prompt.txt)

A reduced stringed instrument with a curled bowl. This explores the classical quality of the reference through music and offers a more abstract alternative to a portrait or mascot.

Proposed palette: ink `#20211C`, warm ivory `#F4F0E7`, and muted indigo `#343E67`.

## The Listener

[Concept image](04-listener.png) · [Generation prompt](04-listener-prompt.txt)

The user's additional reference is [Lofi Girl](https://www.lofigirl.com/). This direction brings the quiet listening ritual and warmth of that world into an original headphone-wearing character, drawn with the strong black-and-white portrait treatment of the Hermes reference. A short wavy bob, lowered chin, and closed eye give the character a calm, compact silhouette.

The board combines the portrait and wordmark with an app-icon study and a painted rainy-window listening scene. Proposed palette: ink `#20211C`, warm ivory `#F4F0E7`, forest `#355A48`, and amber `#C19A63`.

This is the recommended direction for further exploration of the combined references. Its loose hair strands and facial details need reduction for menu-bar use; the miniature on the board is illustrative, not an actual-size acceptance check.

## Status

These are raster concept studies generated with the built-in image generation tool. Colors in the images are illustrative; the values above are intended design tokens. The exact prompts are saved beside the images. The plates show a large mark, wordmark, palette, and small reversed icon treatment for comparison.

A selected direction still needs vector drawing, optical refinement, a licensed typeface selection, and actual-size checks for the macOS menu bar and app icon. The current app icon and application source are unchanged by this exploration.
