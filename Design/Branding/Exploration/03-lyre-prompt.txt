Use case: logo-brand.
Create one refined horizontal 4:3 identity concept plate for "geet", a quiet native Mac music player. Geet means song in Hindi and Urdu. Design an original classical music emblem influenced by the stark black-and-white reduction, timeless graphic strength, and sculptural economy of a classical cameo, without copying any existing logo.
Subject: a small lyre reduced into an ownable single-color emblem. Two elegant outward-curving sculptural arms, exactly three strong vertical strings, and a sweeping curled bowl that subtly suggests a lowercase g. Intelligent warm geometry and optical balance. The emblem must read as a lyre immediately while rewarding a second look with the g. Three to five broad solid shapes, very clean negative spaces, no thin fragile details.
Composition: generous warm ivory #F4F0E7 canvas, huge ink #20211C emblem centered in the upper middle, elegant lowercase serif wordmark "geet" beneath it with comfortable spacing and a warm editorial character. One tiny reversed ivory emblem inside a muted indigo #343E67 rounded-square app icon in the lower right. Three small palette circles in ink, ivory with a hairline outline, and indigo at lower left. Main emblem and inset emblem must be identical.
Style: flat vector-like solid geometry, exceptionally crisp edges, sophisticated identity studio proof, sparse and calm, abundant whitespace, readable at thumbnail scale.
Text verbatim: "geet". No other text.
Avoid: headings, eyebrows, labels, annotations, taglines, grids, decorative leaves, crowns, crests, treble clefs, floating notes, headphones, gradients, shadows, textures, borders, mockup objects, fake UI.
